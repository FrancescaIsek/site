EventMe

UI/UX case study.

EventMe is an app that is committed to improving the experience of events and ticket purchasing. The app will streamline event discovery, provide personalized recommendations, enhance the user interface, prioritize safety, and facilitate ticket purchases. The ultimate objective is to deliver a seamless and delightful ticket buying experience for every user.

Project
Event ticketing app
Date
October 2023
Scope
Research, UX & UI design

01

Overview

The Problem

Today, using event ticketing apps can be difficult and not ideal for many people. These apps often have confusing interfaces, making it hard to find events they like. They don’t personalize suggestions, so you have to search through lots of events. Safety is also a concern, with worries about your data and payment security. Additionally, these apps don’t have features that make buying tickets fun and interactive.

The Solution

So, there is a need for an event ticketing app that makes things easier for users, gives personalized suggestions, and ensures secure transactions. This app should also have features that make users more engaged and satisfied, solving the challenges they currently face.

03

User research

To gain a deeper understanding of my users, I conducted a comprehensive screener survey to identify potential participants who would be interested in participating in interviews.

My target audience for this study consisted of adults aged between 18 and 55. This age group was selected because they represent a significant portion of individuals who have a strong affinity for attending various events, including parties, concerts, shows, and more. It is widely observed that people within this age range are more inclined to purchase event tickets online due to its inherent advantages, such as convenience, speed, and ease of use. By targeting this specific demographic, I was able to establish a sample group that accurately represents the preferences and behaviors of event-goers who frequently rely on online platforms to purchase their tickets.

Research Goals

By researching prospective users, I hoped to learn, identify and gain;

  • Understand the needs and preferences of potential users in event discovery and ticket.
  • Identify challenges and frustrations users face when using event ticketing platforms.
  • Gain insights into user behaviors and habits for finding and attending events.
  • Determine expected features and functionalities for an event ticketing app.
  • Identify concerns related to data security and payment safety.
  • Learn about user preferences for event types, genres, and locations.

06

Product goals

These product goals are designed to align with the insights gathered from user research. The main objective of EventMe is to offer users a superior experience in discovering events and purchasing tickets, while also addressing their specific needs and concerns.

01

To simplify the process of discovering events by providing a wide range of events and an easy-to-use interface that caters to the diverse interests of users.

02

Implement a personalized recommendation system that suggests events based on users’ preferences and past attendance, enhancing their event discovery experience.

03

Simplify the process of purchasing tickets to reduce frustration during transactions, while ensuring a smooth and efficient user experience.

04

To create an intuitive and enjoyable user experience, prioritize user-centric design principles. This involves focusing on visual appeal, ease of navigation, and simplicity.

07

Competitive analysis

StrengthsWeaknesses

Eventbrite

Strengths

  • Eventbrite is known for its easy-to-use interface, making it simple for event organizers to list events.
  • Offers a wide variety of community-driven and local events.
  • Allows easy sharing and integration with social media platforms.

Weaknesses

  • Eventbrite focuses more on smaller, local events rather than major concerts or sports events.
  • May not be as integrated with larger venues and arenas.

Ticket Master

Strengths

  • Ticketmaster offers access to a wide range of events, making it a comprehensive platform.
  • Offers a wide Well-known and trusted in the event ticketing industry.
  • Often integrated with venue websites, enhancing user convenience for purchasing tickets.

Weaknesses

  • High service fees can increase overall ticket costs.
  • Limited personalized event recommendations compared to competitors.

StubHub

Strengths

  • Offers a secondary marketplace for buying and selling tickets, including sold-out events.
  • User-friendly mobile app for convenient ticket purchases.
  • Provides access to various events, including major sports events and concerts.

Weaknesses

  • StubHub primarily focuses on ticket resale, with less emphasis on event discovery and personalization.

UI design

Splash Screen and
Onboarding Screen

  • The Splash Screen prominently displays the EventMe app’s logo, enhancing brand recognition and creating a strong first impression.
  • The onboarding screen actively engages users by showcasing the app’s value proposition and demonstrating how it can make event discovery and ticket purchasing easier and more enjoyable.

Sign In /
Sign up

The sign up process is easy and straightforward. Users provide their details, create an account. They can choose to sign up using Google, Facebook or Apple.

Returning users will enter their Name and password to access their accounts.

There is an option to reset password for users who can’t remember their login details.

Users can opt to stay signed in on their devices for convenience using the remember me option.

Homepage

After users sign in, they proceed to the home screen. This is a simple user interface design. On the homepage, users can explore events by category, such as music concerts, sports, arts, or food festivals. They can also view the different events in their categories. There is a notifications, and search icon for ease of search.

Event Details page

The Event Details Page is where users can access comprehensive information about a specific event they’re interested in including the date, time, detailed description of the event, artist lineup, venue details, and any special highlights, will be seen there.

Scan Card

During the payment process, users can decide to scan their card rather than manually inputting their details. This is for ease and accessibility.
